# Prediction option catalogue sources

## Research scope and date

Research was completed on **19 August 2026** for season **2026/27**. The fixed 20-club field was checked against the Premier League's [official confirmation of the 2026/27 clubs](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4673099/the-202627-premier-league-season-officially-starts/). The first league match is scheduled for 21 August, according to the [official 2026/27 season dates](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4468487/dates-for-202627-premier-league-season-confirmed).

This is a point-in-time catalogue. The Premier League [summer transfer window](https://www.premierleague.com/en/transfers/2026-27/summer) was still open and was scheduled to close at 23:00 BST on 1 September 2026. Final squad registration therefore had not occurred.

## Cup competitions

### UEFA Champions League

The 43 options are the 29 confirmed league-phase clubs and 14 clubs in active play-off ties on the research date, taken from UEFA's live [2026/27 Champions League teams page](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/clubs/) and checked against UEFA's [qualifying fixtures, results and dates](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/accesslist/). Qualifying was not final: play-off first legs were being played on 18–19 August and second legs were scheduled for 25–26 August. Clubs shown by UEFA only under completed earlier qualifying rounds were excluded.

### UEFA Europa League

The 41 options are the 17 clubs already in the league phase and 24 clubs in active Europa League play-offs, taken from UEFA's live [2026/27 Europa League teams page](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/clubs/) and [qualifying guide](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/accesslist/). The field was not final: play-offs were scheduled for 20 and 27 August. Eliminated clubs left under completed qualifying-round headings were excluded.

### UEFA Conference League

The 48 options are the 48 active play-off participants on UEFA's live [2026/27 Conference League teams page](https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/clubs/), checked against UEFA's [qualifying fixtures and play-off ties](https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/news/02a6-20e5e911587f-cc10425958b3-1000--conference-league-qualifying-fixtures-dates-how-it-works/). The field was not final: the ties were scheduled for 20 and 27 August, and the 12 Europa League play-off losers had not yet transferred into the league phase. Clubs shown only under completed earlier rounds were excluded.

### FA Cup

The 743 options reproduce the FA's complete [official list of 743 accepted clubs](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/competitions/2026-27/accepted-exemptions-and-prize-fund/020726/the-emirates-fa-cup---list-of-clubs-accepted.ashx). The FA's [entries announcement](https://www.thefa.com/news/2026/jun/26/clubs-accepted-exemptions-mens-fa-cup-fa-youth-fa-trophy-fa-vase-2026-27) confirms that this is the accepted field. This catalogue intentionally records official entrants, not only clubs still alive on 19 August; the [extra preliminary round](https://thefa.com/competitions/thefacup/fixtures) had already begun.

### Carabao Cup

The 92 options are the full 92-club Premier League/EFL field. The exact 20 Premier League, 24 Championship and 48 League One/League Two clubs were cross-checked against the FA's official [2026/27 exemption list](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/thefacom-new/files/competitions/2026-27/accepted-exemptions-and-prize-fund/the-emirates-fa-cup---list-of-exemptions.ashx). Participation and early-round status were checked against the EFL's [2026/27 Carabao Cup competition page](https://www.efl.com/competitions/carabao-cup/) and [Round One draw](https://www.efl.com/news/2026/june/25/carabao-cup-round-one-draw-confirmed/). The list retains every entrant, including clubs eliminated before the research date.

## Young Player eligibility

The latest season-specific official wording says the award is for the best player who was **aged 21 or younger at the start of the season**; see the Premier League's [Young Player award history updated 23 May 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2469110) and the [2025/26 nominee criteria](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4656525/vote-for-your-young-player-of-the-season). The separate evergreen [awards overview](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4337263/premier-league-awards) still says 23 or younger, creating an official-site conflict.

No dedicated 2026/27 nomination terms had been published by 19 August. This catalogue therefore applies the newer, season-specific **21-or-younger** wording to the official [21 August 2026 opening date](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4468487/dates-for-202627-premier-league-season-confirmed). Eligible players are those born **after 21 August 2004** (22 August 2004 or later). This cutoff should be rechecked when the 2026/27 award terms or shortlist are published.
